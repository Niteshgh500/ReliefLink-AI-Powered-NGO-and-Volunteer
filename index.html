Process

From Task to Completion in Minutes

A streamlined 4-step flow that connects need with action — fast.

🏢
01

NGO Posts Task

NGO describes the task, sets urgency level, required skills, and location. AI can also digitize paper requests automatically.

🤖
02

AI Ranks Volunteers

Smart engine scores all available volunteers by proximity, skill match, past reliability, and current availability in real-time.

✅
03

Volunteer Accepts

Top-ranked volunteers receive a push notification. One tap accepts the task with estimated arrival time calculated automatically.

📸
04

Task Completed with Proof

Volunteer uploads photo proof, updates status, and earns trust score points. NGO receives instant confirmation.
